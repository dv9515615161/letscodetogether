RideScore Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

RideScore reads ride offers shown by Rapido Captain and Uber Driver and tells the driver what an offer is worth per hour and per kilometre. This policy describes exactly what the app accesses and what happens to it.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. RideScore does not request the Android internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

What the app accesses

  • Screen text from two apps only. Through Android's AccessibilityService, RideScore reads the text of the foreground screen when that app is Rapido Captain or Uber Driver: fare, distances, times, and pickup and drop addresses. The package of the app in front is checked before any screen content is requested, so no other app's screen is read.
  • Screen pixels, only if you switch on the OCR fallback. This is off by default. When on, and only when the offer text cannot be read directly, the app captures one frame and recognises text on it using an on-device model. Android shows its own recording indicator while this is active. Captured frames are held in memory, read, and discarded immediately.
  • The settings you enter — mileage, petrol price, thresholds.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings, in the app's private storage on the device.
  • The ride log, only if you switch it on. Off by default. When on, each offer seen is appended to a CSV file in the app's private storage, which no other app can read. It is capped in size and trimmed automatically. It leaves your phone only if you press Export and choose where to send it. You can delete it at any time from Settings.

Nothing else is written to disk. Screen text that is not logged exists only in memory while an offer is being scored.

What is never collected

  • No account, sign-in, name, phone number or email.
  • No location. RideScore does not request location permission.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising identifiers.
  • No screenshots are saved.
  • No data is sold, shared or transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What the app cannot do

RideScore never accepts, declines, taps, swipes or scrolls anything in Rapido or Uber. Its accessibility service is declared without the ability to perform actions in other apps, so this is enforced by the Android configuration rather than only promised here. Every decision is the driver's.

Permissions and why

  • Accessibility service — to read the offer text. This is the app's core function and there is no other way to do it. You enable it yourself in Android Settings and can revoke it there at any time.
  • Display over other apps — to show the floating advisory card.
  • Notifications — to show the verdict when the card cannot be drawn, such as on the lock screen.
  • Foreground service / media projection — only used by the optional OCR fallback, which Android requires to run as a foreground service.
  • No internet permission is requested.

Children

RideScore is a tool for working drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that sends data off the device, it will be opt-in, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app.
